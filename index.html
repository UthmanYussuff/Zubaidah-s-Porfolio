BNY MellonOutside counsel management

Law firm convergence project

BNY set out to consolidate its external legal provider base from more than 200 firms to 100 and reduce external legal spend. I reviewed Premier and panel firms by region and specialism, alongside their portfolios of open and closed matters, including those awaiting conclusion or payment.

I worked closely with senior stakeholders, in-house counsel and relationship managers to understand existing firm relationships and assess where matters could be consolidated without disrupting legal support or exposing the bank to additional risk. This included identifying opportunities to direct more work to the bank's four Premier firms to benefit from negotiated fee arrangements.
